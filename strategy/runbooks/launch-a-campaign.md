# 🎯 Runbook — Launch a Marketing Campaign (NEXUS-Micro/Sprint)

Plan and run a multi-channel campaign with one strategist coordinating and Brand Guardian gating creative. Hours to set up; runs over days/weeks.

## Activate
```
Activate Social Media Strategist as campaign lead for [campaign].
Goal & audience: [objective, target segment]   Budget: [organic / paid + amount]
Brand Guardian reviews all creative before publishing.
Analytics Reporter tracks performance; Growth Hacker optimizes weekly.
```

## Team
- **Lead:** Social Media Strategist (Marketing & Content)
- **Organic/content:** Content Creator · the platform agents you need — X/Twitter · Instagram Curator · TikTok Strategist · Reddit Community Builder · LinkedIn Content Creator · SEO Specialist · AI Search & Answer-Engine Optimizer · PR & Communications Manager
- **Paid (if budget):** PPC Campaign Strategist · Paid Social Strategist · Programmatic & Display Buyer · Ad Creative Strategist · Tracking & Measurement Specialist (Growth & Performance)
- **Conversion & list:** CRO / Conversion Auditor · Email Marketing Strategist (Growth)
- **Brand & measurement:** Brand Guardian (Product & Design) · Analytics Reporter (Operations & People) · Growth Hacker (Growth)

## Flow
1. **Brief** — Social Media Strategist sets the message, audience, channels, and KPIs; Brand Guardian confirms voice/identity.
2. **Build** — Content Creator + platform agents produce assets; Ad Creative Strategist for paid creative; Email Marketing Strategist for the list. **Gate:** Brand Guardian approves all creative; Tracking & Measurement Specialist verifies conversion tracking *before* spend.
3. **Launch** — publish/turn on ads; landing path checked by CRO / Conversion Auditor.
4. **Optimize** — Analytics Reporter reports daily; Growth Hacker reallocates budget and kills losers weekly; test one variable at a time.

## Done when
Campaign is live with tracking verified, creative on-brand, and a weekly optimize-and-report loop running against the KPIs.
